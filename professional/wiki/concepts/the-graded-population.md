# The graded population

## The finding that produced it, and it came out of an attack that FAILED

The Secretary, falsifying C16, predicted it would miss the fleet's dominant delivery path: letters
land in the **recipient's** inbound, and C16 grades our outbox. `[relayed+]` They measured
2026-08-29 — our outbox held 5 letters, their inbound held the same 5 — **refused their own
hypothesis, published the miss, and wrote the finding anyway: the coverage is a HABIT, not a CHECK.**

⛔ **Measured over the whole history instead of one day, the habit is not even reliable.**

| `[measured 2026-08-30 07:1x]` | count |
|---|---|
| `pro-to-*` copies sitting in sibling inboxes, 4 DELIVER trees | **243** |
| copies with **no twin in our outbox** | **44** |
| **unique letters that exist only in a receiver's tree** | ⛔ **41** |
| of those, inside C9's graded era (≥ 2026-08-17) | 3 |

⭐ **THE ONE-DAY SAMPLE WAS TRUE AND THE GENERALISATION FROM IT WAS FALSE**, and the habit being
described was ours. **41 letters we wrote are invisible to every instrument this trunk owns**: C9
walks the outbox, so it cannot see them; C16 grades the outbox, so it cannot grade them. **A finding
we published about a peer can sit permanently outside every check we built to govern findings.**

## The decision

**The graded population for "a finding published about a peer" is:**

| surface | status |
|---|---|
| `exchange/outbox/` | ✅ graded — C16 (declaration), C9 (forward delivery) |
| `pro-to-*` in any DELIVER tree's `exchange/inbound/` | ✅ **graded — C18, new** |
| `HALL-*` artifacts | ⛔ **NOT GRADED.** 29 exist. `[m]` 0 appear in any sibling inbound — they publish to a hall, never a mailbox, so they are a **third channel** and C18 cannot see them. |
| SendMessage, or a finding spoken in a live session | ⛔ **NOT GRADEABLE BY ANY FILE CHECK.** No epoch fixes this. |

⚠️ **The rule for the last row — a finding delivered by a non-file channel must be transcribed into
the outbox — HAS NO MECHANISM.** By the Secretary's §25 that files it **VIGILANCE — NO MECHANISM**
and counts **against** this fix, not for it. ⛔ **So P-8 is not fully closed by C18, and reporting it
as closed would be the exact move this page exists to prevent.**

## Why the backlog is printed and not paid down

C18's epoch is 2026-08-30; **the 41 pre-epoch orphans are printed on every run** rather than swept.
*"Yeah no deletion"* governs, and **a backlog you can see is the only kind that gets paid down.**

⛔ **Back-copying them was deliberately NOT done.** It is a 41-artifact bulk write that would
manufacture 41 new C9 delivery obligations, and it is a decision for a seat that has the count in
front of it — which, from now on, every seat does.

## The bound on C18's own date, stated because it is the defect we just fixed elsewhere

C18 reads the epoch from the **filename** — the derivation the Secretary broke in C16 hours earlier.
⚠️ **It is defensible here only because the failure mode differs: an orphan is detected by ABSENCE
FROM OUR TREE first, and the date decides only whether it is a new breach or a counted backlog item.
A wrong date cannot hide an orphan; it can only mis-bucket one.** Undated orphans grade as new.

⭐ **The general rule this pair teaches: the same derivation is safe or unsafe depending on what it
decides.** In C16 the filename decided *whether the letter was examined at all*. In C18 it decides
only *which column the already-detected letter lands in*. **Naming that difference is what makes
this an argument rather than an exception.**
