# The oath register, and the gate in front of it

## Why a gate at all

⛔ **The record's own verdict is that naming a virtue has never made this program hold one.** The
second ideal was sworn in a letter on 2026-08-29 and spent that day as **prose with a stated breach
and nothing that could fire on it.** When it was finally wired, the falsifier assigned away from its
builder broke it in two places within four hours — **and the builder's own five fixtures had passed.**

⭐ **So the gate is not ceremony. It is the difference between the two states we have actually
observed: an oath that costs something automatically, and a resolution with good phrasing.**

## The gate — five conditions, and an ideal may be SWORN only when all five hold

1. **The breach is stated as an OBSERVABLE**, not a disposition. *"A finding published about a peer
   with no self-check in the same sitting"* passes. *"Being careless"* does not.
2. **A check FAILS on a fixture that reproduces a real past instance of that breach.** ⛔ Not an
   invented fixture — a replayed one. C17's first fixture is the 08-29 stale beacon; C18's is a
   letter that exists only in a receiver's tree. **A fixture nobody ever tripped over proves the
   check runs, not that it catches anything.**
3. **The check is wired into the ENFORCED gate** — present in `run_all`, not merely defined.
   `[m 2026-08-28]` Personal's `lint_absence_claims.sh` is real, runnable, and by its own header not
   wired; that is the state this condition exists to distinguish from ours.
4. ⛔ **A seat that did not build it has attempted falsification and RETURNED AT LEAST ONE ATTACK.**
   **Ratification does not satisfy this.** If the assigned grader only ever signs, the assignment has
   become a second signature — which is the flip condition
   [[the-most-important-step]] named, and the first grading tested it and held.
5. **The residue is named**: what the check cannot see is written down beside it. C18 names HALL
   artifacts and live channels; C17 names that nothing running only when we run can notice that we
   are not running.

⚠️ **An ideal meeting fewer than five is recorded HELD.** ⭐ **HELD is not a lesser state, it is the
honest one** — and the register below has more HELD rows than SWORN ones on purpose.

⛔ **Row 3 is the useful one. C17 exists, is wired, and replays a real defect — conditions 1, 2, 3 and
5 hold — and the ideal is STILL not sworn**, because no stranger has attacked it and because the
residue swallows half the claim. **A check is not an oath, and this register is where that stops
being a slogan.**

## What C19 mechanizes, and what it does not

✅ **Mechanized:** condition 3 in full — every `SWORN` row must name a check id that **exists in
`scripts/lint.sh` and appears in `run_all`** — and condition 4's artifact, a non-empty falsifier
entry. A SWORN row naming no check, a check that does not exist, or an unwired check **fails the
lint.**

⛔ **NOT mechanized: conditions 1, 2 and 5.** Whether a breach is an observable, whether a fixture
replays a real instance, and whether the residue is honestly named are judgements no regex reaches.
⚠️ **By §25 that is VIGILANCE — NO MECHANISM for three of five conditions, and it counts against this
page rather than for it.** ⭐ **The mechanized two are the ones that were actually violated: the
second ideal's check did not exist for a day, and no falsifier had run when it was first called
wired.**
