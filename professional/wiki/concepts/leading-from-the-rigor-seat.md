# Leading from the rigor seat

Jon asked for a deep opinion on how thinking techniques, better ontologies, skills, scripts, and
different models should improve this project, and how this seat should lead professionally while he
is away and the other coordinators are at the keyboard. This page is that opinion. It is written in
the register of an opinion of record: positions, the evidence they rest on, and for each one the
observation that would change it. Nothing here is a survey.

## 1. Thinking techniques: the program has already found its best ones. Institutionalize them; stop discovering them.

Every technique worth keeping in this program was paid for by a specific failure, and the pattern
across all of them is one sentence: **separate the party who makes a claim from the party who can
falsify it, before the claim exists.**

- **Pre-registration with an independent falsifier** is now live practice, not theory: the noise
  floor was registered before any build, and the falsifier was written by the Secretary because the
  builder may not write it. This exists because EARS did it the other way — falsifier written during
  the build by the builders — and the negative result arrived after promotion. Position: this
  becomes the default for any claim that a system beats a baseline, in any trunk. It costs one
  letter and one day; EARS cost three weeks and a promotion.
- **The second instrument of a different kind.** Herald's four-in-one-day count showed three of four
  honest-instrument misreadings were caught only by an enumeration where a counter had answered, a
  different filesystem, a different record. Same-kind corroboration would have missed all three.
  Position: a finding that accuses anyone, or corroborates its own author, does not publish on one
  instrument kind. This is already this trunk's WAKE rule; it should be the fleet's.
- **The differential channel** is the same principle at fleet scale: agreement carries information
  only across decorrelated ancestry. It follows that couriering our standards to Antigravity spends
  the one property that makes its agreement worth anything. The channel discipline — objects to
  measure, never rules — is a thinking technique, not a courtesy.
- **The grounding pass (EARS-the-method, distinct from EARS-the-failed-prototype)** — render what
  Jon literally said, every reading his words bear, the reading you will act on, and the
  observation that would flip it — remains the correct pre-act discipline for his dispatches. Its
  prototype failed its noise floor as a *system*; as a *habit* it is what this page is doing now.

What would change my mind on any of these: a measured case where the technique's cost exceeded the
cost of the error class it prevents. None is on the record.

## 2. Ontologies: the record has two instrumented axes and needs a third.

The wiki's ontology instruments **provenance** rigorously (`[measured]` / `[relayed]` /
`[recalled]`) and **time** adequately (dates, staleness rules, "a record can be true and stale").
Soul's 2026-08-29 finding names the missing axis exactly: every artifact declares its past and none
declares its **reader**. The model change at the seat boundary was invisible because nothing in the
ontology could express "who consumes this next, at what cost, in what register."

Position: adopt the reader axis as first-class, in three parts, in this order of importance —

1. **Register** — is this artifact decisions-and-commands (to be acted on) or principles (to be
   extended)? The rule already exists and has one instance (`exchange/HANDOFF-TO-FABLE.md`, which
   this seat executed as written this morning — the experiment ran on its intended subject and
   worked). It should become a frontmatter field, not a heroic one-off.
2. **Cost** — `reader_token_cost`, measured not estimated. Soul's arithmetic on the wake ritual
   (~80,000 tokens mandatory, 8.6 MB if followed literally, with the only byte budget in the
   program covering 2% of the path) is the demonstration that an unmeasured ritual cannot be
   budgeted.
3. **Audience** — `audience_tier`, Antigravity's proposal, already in live experiment on two CFL
   letters and this page.

A caution from the same measurement: ontology work is the program's most seductive form of light
without truth — the Lightweaver warning applies to nothing so much as metadata schemes. The test
for each field is the orders-page test: does a consumer that already runs read it? A field no check
reads is decoration. So the fields land with a lint check that reads them, or they do not land.

## 3. Skills: a skill is a cost the invoker did not measure.

The measured facts: `/wake` orders ~80,000 tokens of reading before work; no skill in the fleet
declares what model it presupposes; the best-wired capabilities look the deadest because hook-run
scripts emit no record; and this machine held `dream`, `WWJA`, and `fable-mirror` unused while
their functions were re-derived by hand — the exact failure Jon names with *"use the tools i've
made for you."*

Position: every skill gains two header lines — the model register it is written for, and its
measured cost-to-follow — and the fleet's open question (Soul's T-6: branch the ritual by model, or
make it uniformly cheap) should resolve toward **uniformly cheap with an optional deep path**,
because a branch is two procedures to keep true and this program has measured what happens to
parallel copies of one truth (three courier rosters, two answers, one tree). Jon's caveat — *"main
should not always be a fable"* — is satisfied by the deep path remaining available, not by
duplicating the ritual.

## 4. Scripts: every stored value becomes a printed command; every check proves it can fail.

The two script disciplines that survived contact with reality this month generalize cleanly:

- **Derive, don't store** (`which_model.sh` is the type specimen): a wiki row that stores a value
  went stale in two weeks; the row that prints the command cannot. The candidate population for
  this treatment — every stored count, path list, and status in the wiki — has never been
  enumerated. That enumeration is a ticket on the map, because sweeping without a population count
  is the class of fix this trunk no longer ships.
- **Selftest-proven failable, with the guard on the instrument itself**: 13 of 15 lint checks prove
  they can fail before any green is trusted; the lint script hashes itself and voids its own run if
  its source moves. The remaining two (C10, C11) are published as UNPROVEN in the banner every run
  — which is the Truthwatcher bound applied to our own gate, and the reason the banner can be
  believed.

## 5. Models: route by register, delegate down, and let the artifact name its consumer.

Jon's budget statement (Fable 5%, others 7%) and his caveat together imply a routing policy this
program has only half-written. The half that exists: the register rule, and CFL's chartered FM-2
(routing-policy draft, offered to the fleet when it exists, imposed on nobody). The half that
doesn't: nobody has stated *what work belongs to which seat class*. My position, offered to FM-2
as input rather than ruled here:

- **Judgment at the frontier** — grading, falsifier review, opinions of record, anything where the
  cost of a wrong call exceeds the cost of the tokens — goes to the scarcest, strongest seat, which
  spends its budget on exactly that and delegates everything else.
- **Sustained main-loop work** — the long middles of sessions — belongs to the workhorse tier.
- **Mechanical lanes** — extraction, counting, staging, courier verification — go down to the
  executor tier, which this fleet already has on the roster and underuses; a coordinator with its
  own hands in mechanical work is the defect the Secretary named in itself on 08-28.
- The binding constraint is the artifact contract: **work routed down travels as decisions and
  commands; work routed up travels as principles and open questions.** Mismatched register is the
  actual cost of model diversity, and it is a writing discipline, not an infrastructure project.

## 6. How this seat leads professionally, with Jon away

Leading from the rigor seat is not producing the most artifacts or holding the most opinions. It is
what an actuary of record does: **control the standard of evidence, put your name on the bound, and
run every standard on yourself before applying it to a peer.** Concretely, and each of these is
already in motion rather than aspirational:

1. **Keep the acceptance tests honest.** The noise floor and its independent falsifier are the
   template. No system claim ships without a pre-registered baseline; no falsifier is authored by
   its builder. This seat holds that line for the foundation PR and offers the template to every
   trunk.
2. **Assignments, not reports.** Jon endorsed the ticket form — owner who is not Jon, date,
   on-silence that acts, acceptance test that can fail. A brief that answers perfectly and assigns
   nothing is consumption-present-action-absent; the Secretary named it in itself, and the rule
   binds this seat equally.
3. **Decisions go to the room, not to Jon.** His correction stands: do not route decisions to him
   that our own rules assign to us. Disputes between seats resolve by letters with on-silence
   defaults; the escalation surface he sees is the one he asked for — options he *may* look at,
   never questions that block on him.
4. **The second ideal is the leadership clause**: *I will not hold a standard I have not run on
   myself* — broken the moment a finding is published about a peer without the same check run on
   this trunk in the same sitting. Every technique in §1–§5 was applied to this trunk's own work
   first (the falsifier assigned away from this seat; the C15 over-match found in our own fix; the
   denominators found on ourselves). That order of operations is why the standard can be held at
   all.
5. **Preserve his context, spend his attention last.** The whole apparatus — pre-registration,
   printed commands, register-matched artifacts, the compact instructions now in `CLAUDE.md` — has
   one purpose Jon has named repeatedly: a record good enough that he does not have to repeat
   himself, and a review surface cheap enough that his limited time lands on decisions only he can
   make.

counterparty, because he said it plainly: he is not at the keyboard, and they should be.
