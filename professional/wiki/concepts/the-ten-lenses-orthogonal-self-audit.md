# The ten lenses — an orthogonal self-audit

**Jon, 2026-08-24:** *"Think on the orders of knights radiant and the oaths they might swear in this
context - this is not perfect, but **I know that you each are missing multiple things in roughly
orthoginal directions** and my hope is that by opining deeply you might better think roughly
orthoginally to nitice the GIANT PILE OF BULLSHIT you would leave for me."*

⭐ **HE HAD ALREADY USED THE FRAME AND NOBODY NOTICED.** The First Ideal is *"Life before death,
strength before weakness, **journey before destination**."* **On 2026-08-23 he asked this trunk:
*"What is your journey?"* and on 2026-08-22 he closed a message to Personal with *"Strength."***
⛔ **We answered "what is your journey" as a rhetorical prompt for a retrospective. It was the second
half of an oath, and the whole point of that clause is that THE JOURNEY IS THE THING BEING GRADED,
not the destination.** ⚠️ **A program that reports only destinations — closed tickets, green lints —
is failing the ideal its user was quoting at it.**

## Why lenses and not virtues

⛔ **This page is useless as inspiration and is not written as any.** Every defect this program found
in the 48 hours before it was written was found by **a lens someone happened to be holding**, never
by a lens anyone chose: a flat `ls` found a false accusation because someone re-searched by content;
a stale refusal surfaced because a peer asked an unrelated question about writability; a divergent
duplicate pack surfaced because a **timed-out** search returned late.

⭐ **That is not diligence. That is luck with good record-keeping.** **The orders are useful here for
exactly one reason: they are ORTHOGONAL. Ten questions that fail differently will not all miss the
same defect** — which is the whole of Jon's sentence, and it is a credibility argument, not a
literary one. ⚠️ **Ten correlated checks are `n = 1` wearing `n = 10`; that is the same error as
four of our own pages agreeing.**

## ⭐ The one that indicts this program hardest, and it is not close

⛔ **EDGEDANCER. *"I will remember those who have been forgotten. I will listen to those who have
been ignored."*** **Every large defect in this trunk's log is an arrival nobody read**, and the four
instances above are not related by cause — different trunks, different mechanisms, different years of
code. **They are related by the absence of a reader.**

⭐ **And the sharpest version is not about letters at all: JON'S OWN WORDS WERE ON THIS DISC, UNREAD,
WHILE WE RE-DERIVED HIS POSITION FROM AN ACTUARIAL STANDARD.** `[measured]` He paired materiality
with professionalism himself on 2026-08-20; we derived that pairing from ASOP 1 §2.6 on 2026-08-23
without knowing. **He asked for the dream skill on 2026-05-09 — 107 days ago — and `grep -ri dream`
across every `skills/` tree returns zero files.**

⛔ **THAT IS THE PILE HE IS TALKING ABOUT.** Not wrong work. **Unread work, and unbuilt asks, in a
program whose entire subject is his context.**

## How to actually run this

**It is ten questions and it costs about two minutes. Run it at a wake, and before shipping anything
to Jon.** ⚠️ **Not all ten every time — that becomes ceremony and ceremony is what he is complaining
about.** ⭐ **Run the three that are ORTHOGONAL TO THE WORK IN HAND, deliberately chosen because they
are the ones the current task would not surface on its own.** ⛔ **If a lens returns nothing, that is
a result and it is worth one line — a lens that has never returned anything is either wrong or
never actually run, and both are worth knowing.**

⚠️ **The failure mode of this page is that it becomes a badge.** ⭐ **Its own Skybreaker test:
*if this checklist passes, what exactly has been proven?* **Nothing, unless each lens names a
measurement.** A lens answered from memory is worse than a lens skipped, because it produces a
receipt.
