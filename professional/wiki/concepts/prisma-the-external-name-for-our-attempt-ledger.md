## The external source, named

**PRISMA 2020** — *Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analyses*. A 27-item
checklist plus a flow diagram, in use since 2009 and revised in 2020, and the near-universal
reporting standard for evidence synthesis across medicine and the social sciences.
`[m 2026-08-24 22:3x]` **Zero hits for "prisma" anywhere in this trunk. Siblings NOT SEARCHED.**

**What its flow diagram requires** (`prisma-statement.org`, fetched): it *"maps out the number of
records identified, included and excluded, **and the reasons for exclusions**."*
**Checklist item 16a:** *"Describe the results of the search and selection process, from the number
of records identified in the search to the number of studies included in the review, ideally using a
flow diagram."*
⭐ **And the requirement that does the work: at the eligibility stage you report the total excluded
AND list each reason WITH the count excluded for that reason.**

## ⛔ THE DIFF, AND IT IMPLICATES TONIGHT'S OWN RUN

**We report SURVIVORS. PRISMA reports the FLOW.** Our sweeps say *"6 orphans"*; PRISMA says you have
not reported a search until you have said how many you started with, how many you dropped, and
**why, with a count per reason.**

⚠️ **Graded against tonight's own dream run, honestly:**

| tonight's sweep | did it report the PRISMA flow? |
|---|---|
| orphans, 12 → 6 | ✅ **yes** — 12 identified, 6 excluded, each with its reason named |
| cold probe, 10 → 8 → 2 | ✅ **yes** — and the six declines each carry a reason |
| **term diff, 21 candidates** | ⛔ **NO.** The threshold (≥8 mentions) was printed; **the POPULATION was not.** How many coinages were considered, and how many fell below the bar, was never published — so *"21 candidates"* is an included-count with no identified-count. |

⭐ **That is exactly the failure PRISMA's identification box exists to prevent, found in our own
work by applying a standard we had never read.** ⛔ **`[[a-count-of-what-you-read-is-unaudited]]`
generalises: a count of what SURVIVED is unauditable without the count of what was CONSIDERED.**

## Why this is the highest-value single import available

⛔ **`wiki/concepts/negative-claims-require-an-attempt-ledger.md` is a page with no artifact.**
`[m]` the phrase appears nowhere outside that page and six letters — **we wrote the rule, shipped it
to four trunks, and never built the thing.** ⭐ **PRISMA is that thing, already specified, already
adopted by an entire research culture, with a template and a generator.** **We do not have to
design it. We have to stop re-deriving it.** See [[re-derived-not-researched]].

⚠️ **What does NOT transfer, stated so the import is not oversold:** PRISMA governs *reporting*, not
*searching* — it will not tell us WHICH sources to consult, only that we must say which we did and
what each yielded. **It is a completeness discipline, not a coverage one.** Its unit is also a
*study*, not a *grep*; the mapping onto corpus sweeps is ours to make and is untested.

## The standing rule this produces

⛔ **PUBLISH THE FLOW, NOT THE SURVIVORS: identified → excluded (reason + count, each) → included.**
✅ **A sweep that reports only what it found has not reported its search.**
