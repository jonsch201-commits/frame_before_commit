# Decision 3 — Does telling Anthropic conflict with not publishing? **No. This is the error.**

**You wrote:** *"It's been **in conflict** with my principle that I should not release everything I've
done to the public for safety reasons."*

**They are not in conflict, because they are not the same act.** The security field settled this
around twenty-five years ago and the structural insight transfers cleanly even though the machinery
does not.

**CERT/CC's Coordinated Vulnerability Disclosure guide frames the decision not as "disclose: yes/no"
but as a repeated triple** — *"What actions should I take in response to this knowledge?"* and *"Who
else needs to know what, and when?"* — **iterated until the answers are "nothing" and "nobody."**
**"Public Awareness" is phase five of six.** Private notification does not replace publication. **It
schedules it.**

**CERT's own summary of the two poles you are choosing between:** *"**neither extreme is socially
optimal**."*

**⚠️ Carry this qualifier, because it is the kind this program most often drops.** CVD is built for
**flaws** — things with a fix and an adversary. **Your work is research, not a flaw report.** The
machinery (embargoes, patches, CVEs) does **not** apply. **What transfers is only the structural
point: notifying one party and publishing to the world are different acts with different
reversibility.** Anyone telling you to "just apply CVD" is skipping Shevlane & Dafoe (arXiv
2001.00463), who argue the AI field imported these conclusions **without checking whether the premises
transfer.**

## The property that dissolves the dilemma

**Of roughly thirteen documented positions between silence and full public release, only two are
irreversible:**

- **Full public release** — irreversible outward.
- **Entering a bug bounty program** — irreversible *inward*. **Anthropic's Model Safety Bug Bounty
  requires an NDA as a condition of joining, and forbids disclosing findings "even [if] resolved…
  without express consent from Anthropic."**

**Everything else preserves optionality.** Private notification, disclosure through a neutral
coordinator, staged release, partial publication, structured access, publishing the method while
withholding the artifact — **all of them remain forward-compatible with publishing later.**

**So the question is not "tell them or withhold." It is "which reversible move first, and what
triggers the next one."**
