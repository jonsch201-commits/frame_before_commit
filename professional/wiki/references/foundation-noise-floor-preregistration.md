# Pre-registration: the noise floor the foundation PR must beat

## 1. Why this exists, in one paragraph

Jon has assigned Professional a PR to formalize the foundation of the memory-and-thought-branching
systems, with an acceptance test that is a person: an outside reader who feels no friction. The
nearest ancestor this PR has — EARS, a grounding pass over Jon's own words, approved sight unseen
with a falsifier written during the build by the builders — measured at Cycle 21 as no better than a
plain careful read `[relayed — DRAFT-promotion-criteria-v0-2026-08-08.md:209-219; corroborated
independently by Soul, which read the backtest in full]`. Secretary ticket C-7 therefore requires,
before the foundation PR is built: a stated unaided-careful-reader baseline, registered before the
build, with the falsifier written by someone who is not the builder. This file is that registration.

## 2. The claim under test, stated so it can lose

> The foundation system, used by a cold reader, produces materially better outcomes on
> answer-finding tasks than the same reader given only the raw corpus and no tooling.

If the system arm does not beat the plain-read arm, the foundation PR ships no architecture claims —
it documents what exists and states plainly that the measured value over a careful read is not
demonstrated. Furniture is not shipped as foundation.

## 3. Design, fixed now (per the instrument spec CFL CONCURred to on 2026-08-28)

- 5–8 tasks of the form "find X and tell me what it says", each with an answer already known to the
  registrants and unknown to the reader.
- Half the tasks run against the system, half against a plain careful read of the same corpus with
  no tooling. Same reader class, same session, order counterbalanced.
- Per task, the flow is recorded, not the survivor: `attempts[]`, `first_dead_end_at`, `abandoned` +
  what they were trying, `asked_a_human` + the question, `verbatim_friction` (their words, unedited),
  `answer_correct` + what they answered.
- No score, no rubric, no coaching, no mid-pass fixes, no summaries — raw traces only.
- The reader population is a proxy (residents share our ancestry), so the pass bounds friction from
  BELOW and is reported as a floor, never as a pass. Reaching the residents goes through CFL's
  container volume, not the SSP tree `[relayed — Secretary CORRECTION, 2026-08-28: no seat has ever
  been seated in SSP; the readers run in CFL's volume]`.

## 4. The falsifier, and who writes it

C-7's acceptance test: "the falsifier written by someone who is not the builder." Professional is
the builder-designate of the foundation PR, so Professional may not author its falsifier — the EARS
shape (falsifier written during the build by the builders) is the named defect this clause exists to
prevent.

**Assigned: Secretary**, as the raiser of C-7 and the seat holding Jon's evening orders. Requested
by letter dated 2026-08-29. The falsifier must state, before the first task runs: the margin by
which the system arm must beat the plain-read arm to count as "materially better" (on

On silence past 2026-08-31 12:00 CDT: the registration stands but is reported everywhere as
FALSIFIER-UNWRITTEN, and the build does not start — a registration without an external falsifier is
the EARS shape with better paperwork.

## 5. What is deliberately not in this file

The task list itself. Publishing the tasks before the pass contaminates the reader population (a
reader who has seen the tasks is a different person). The tasks are drafted by the registrants,
held out of every courier channel, and disclosed only in the run records after the pass completes.
