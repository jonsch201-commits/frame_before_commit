# What the fable mirror is here, and what it must never become

**Jon, 2026-08-23:** *"You should consider what your fable mirror should have as its grounding
context. You should ensure you turn on your fable mirror."*

⭐ **The mirror answers one question and no other: WHAT DID THE claude.ai LAYER SAY?** It reads a
parsed transcript corpus. It has **no live access** to claude.ai and never speaks as it.

⛔ **Four prohibitions, and they are the reason the role exists rather than a coordinator just
grepping:** it never impersonates the claude.ai layer · never ratifies · never flips a status ·
never writes to `wiki/`. **Its only write is an escalation packet to `wiki/intake-triage/`.**

# 1 · Why this trunk needs it, stated as a measured gap and not a preference

⛔ **This trunk's retrieval index reaches 413 files and NOT ONE of them is a claude.ai conversation.**
`[measured 2026-08-23]` The claude.ai corpus is **667 parsed transcripts, 61.8 MB**, and it lives in
another trunk: `claude-foundational-layer/raw/transcripts/claude-ai/`.

⭐ **So every "I searched and found nothing" this trunk has ever written about a Jon utterance was a
statement about 413 files, not about the record.** The global constitution already names this exact
failure — a Jon ruling sat unreachable for twenty minutes in a live sibling session on the same disk,
and the letters channel found it when no instrument could. **The mirror is the instrument that
crosses that boundary on purpose instead of by luck.**

# 2 · The grounding context it must be handed, every time

**A mirror invoked without these five answers the wrong question or answers it with false
confidence.**

1. **CORPUS PATH AND ITS BOUNDS.** `claude-foundational-layer/raw/transcripts/claude-ai/` — 667 files.
   ⚠️ **State what the corpus CANNOT see:** the export carries `extracted_content` for 46 attachments
   but **250 `files[]` references with no content field at all** — filenames with nothing behind them.
   **A search that finds the NAME must not report the file as present.**
2. **THIS TRUNK'S CALIBRATION VOCABULARY, because the mirror's output feeds a record that grades it:**
   `[measured]` = ran it here · `[relayed]` = a subagent measured it · `[recalled]` = memory.
   **UNKNOWN dominates PASS.** A negative claim ships with an **attempt ledger** classing each row
   ABSENT / REFUSED-BY-GATE / UNREACHABLE-FROM-HERE. ⛔ **"I searched and found nothing" is a claim
   about the query, never about the world.**
3. **JON'S WORDS ARE QUOTED VERBATIM, TYPOS INTACT, WITH THE CONTEXT THAT MAKES THEM MEAN WHAT HE
   MEANT.** A corrected quote is an unverifiable quote. ⚠️ **A qualified statement reported as
   unqualified is this program's single most repeated error** — carry the qualifier or do not carry
   the quote.
4. **WHAT PROFESSIONAL IS FOR**, so the mirror knows what is relevant: the rigor and credential-stake
   seat; disclosure and publication gate; actuarial-standards holder; `[work]` posture by default.
   ⛔ **Employer-identifying content is gated. Never route live work email, client or claims data.**
5. **THE RETRIEVAL BOUNDARY IT INHERITS.** The embedder here gives typo tolerance and paraphrase **with
   shared vocabulary only** — it does not bridge disjoint vocabulary and it is order-insensitive.
   ⭐ **So the mirror must search the corpus by MULTIPLE PHRASINGS INCLUDING JON'S MISSPELLINGS, not by
   one canonical term.** A single-phrasing search that returns nothing is not evidence.

# 3 · What a good escalation packet contains

Landing path `wiki/intake-triage/` — **created 2026-08-23; it did not exist in this trunk before,
which means the mirror had nowhere to write and was therefore not merely unused but inoperable.**

Each packet: the question as asked · the exact invocations run · what was found with `file:line` ·
**what was searched and NOT found, classed** · verbatim Jon quotes with context · and a **disposition
the coordinator can act on**. ⛔ **No ratification. No status flips. The packet proposes; the seat
disposes.**

# 4 · Standing risk, recorded because it is the failure mode of this specific role

⚠️ **A records-reader that answers fluently is indistinguishable from one that answers correctly,
and it is reporting on a venue its reader cannot check cheaply.** That is why the prohibitions are
structural rather than advisory, and why every packet must carry its invocations — **the invocation
list is what makes the mirror auditable by someone who never opens the corpus.**
