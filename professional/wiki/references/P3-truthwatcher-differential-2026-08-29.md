# P-3 — the Truthwatcher differential

Jon ruled 2026-08-29 (CFL-D-021) that both trunks can hold the order, because an order binds to a
source of radiance rather than a seat. CFL's relay names the consequence: what distinguishes two
members is **which breach conditions are wired to which running instruments.** This is that
enumeration, from the Personal/Herald side, by a read-only lane that adjudicated nothing.

## The finding that lands on US, and it is the one worth having

⛔ **Herald has a RUNNING PROCESS where we have a trigger-question.**
`switchboard-live-watch-v11.sh` is an actual polling watcher — heartbeat file, relay-silence alarm,
dead-lock detection — standing behind their Stoneward oath (*"I will be the thing that notices
absence"*). Our ten-lenses page's Stoneward row is the question *"Does this obligation have a
TRIGGER, or only a date?"* and nothing runs.

⚠️ **And our own WAKE already carries the matching wound**: *"NOTHING WAKES THIS TRUNK. Jon woke it
after 21 hours of silence."* Herald built the instrument for the failure we merely documented.

## The asymmetry in our favour — stated with the qualifier that guts half of it

Both sides have a script that could check an absence-or-measurement claim. `[relayed+]` Personal's
`lint_absence_claims.sh` is real, runnable code that flags an absence pattern lacking a nearby query
marker — **and its own header states it is deliberately NOT wired into `lint.sh`**, because a second
lane gates on that exit code. The lane found no invocation of it in their close ritual's files.

⛔ **This is not licensed as "Herald's oath does not run."** The lane said so itself: the script is
designed for manual invocation, a human or a scheduled job can run it at any time, and Windows Task
Scheduler is invisible to a filesystem search. **"Not found wired into the ritual files I read" is
the finding. "Never runs" is not.**

⚠️ **And the self-application, which is the whole point of the second ideal:** the lane also noted it
did **not** open our `lint.sh` to verify our side of the same claim. Our ten-lenses page is by its
own text a **manual checklist** — *"Run it at a wake, and before shipping anything to Jon"* — so on
the axis that matters (does a ritual document get enforced by anything?) **we are in the same
position we would be describing them as being in.** C16 is one lens wired as of tonight. Nine are
not.
