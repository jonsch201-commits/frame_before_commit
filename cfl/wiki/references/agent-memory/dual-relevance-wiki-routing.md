# Dual-relevance wiki routing

*Source description:* Don't force exclusive FL-vs-personal-vs-pro wiki routing; content is often dual-relevant and FL is home when the chat happened in an FL project

When routing a session/content that spans FL and a personal/home/pro domain, do NOT force a single exclusive destination. Jon (2026-07-12): **"They are all relevant in both places. Chats technically happened in FL."**

**Why:** Sessions run inside FL projects (Triage, agent-framework, etc.) frequently carry soul/family/career content interwoven with FL research. Over-precise "pro or personal?" routing is the wrong frame — the honest answer is usually "both."

**How to apply:**
- The chat's physical home = where it happened (FL project → FL wiki `wiki/sources/`).
- The other domain(s) get **Cross-Wiki references** (a `## Cross-Wiki` section + `[[links]]`), not exclusive ownership. A future Jon-authored personal/soul page references the FL source rather than duplicating it.
- Do NOT hold cross-domain content out of FL as "pending Jon's direction" pointers — capture it where it happened and cross-link. (This corrected an over-restrictive "pointers only, needs direction" framing I'd applied to the 090a56 J-layer page.)
- Parallel precedent: the "wish/secret" shelf-item handling in `agent-interaction-framework-2026-07-02-5990f2` (optional reading tier, convener-authored, cross-referenced — not excluded).
