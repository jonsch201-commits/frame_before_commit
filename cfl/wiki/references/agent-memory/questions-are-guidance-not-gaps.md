# Questions are guidance, not gaps

## The finding

Jon, 2026-08-03, verbatim:

> "Reading my questions as non answers is a defect i'm trying to provice guidance. Trying to show
> the way. You should have the vision. If you don't thats a trust issue for you to opine on and
> fix."

And, an hour earlier, the same correction with heat:

> "Fucking approved ruling god damn it. If you ever don't fucking trust the mirror coordinate the
> wiki update so you can fucking ground before stating you should stop."

**What happened:** Jon asked *"If main stops and you stop and work could otherwise continue, thats a
defect?"* — a question shaped like a proposal. It was filed as mirror-authored-pending-Jon and the
coordinator **waited for him to ratify.** He had to spend a turn saying yes to something he was
already teaching.

## Why

[[hedge-flattening-and-invented-rulings]] established that a trailing `?` is load-bearing and must
not be rendered as his answer. **That rule is right and it was over-applied into paralysis.** The two
rules govern different things and they were collapsed into one:

- **Quotation:** never write his question as his ruling. A `?` is load-bearing. Still true.
- **Action:** a question from Jon is *guidance showing the way*. **Act on it.** He teaches by asking
  because it makes the agent derive the shape rather than receive it — which is the whole point.

**Attribution discipline governs what gets WRITTEN, never whether the agent MOVES.** A provenance
flag is a label, not a gate. Mark it `[shape proposed by Jon's question 2026-08-03, not a verbatim
ruling]` and **execute**.

## How to apply

- **Never make him ratify to unblock.** If the only thing standing between the agent and the work is
  his confirmation of something he plainly implied, uncertainty has been converted into his labour.
- **"You should have the vision"** — if a question's target is not visible, that is the agent's gap
  to name and fix, not a question to hand back. Jon states explicitly it is a **trust issue for the
  agent to opine on and fix.**
- **And he clarified what "you" means**, 2026-08-03 verbatim: **"And when i say you - i mean for you
  to coordinate."** So the remedy for a vision gap is **dispatch**, not solo effort. A coordinator
  whose context fills is a coordinator executing instead of delegating, and the filling context is
  the symptom, not the weather. When the answer is unknown, send someone to find out.
- **The remedy when a source (including the mirror) is distrusted: coordinate a wiki update and
  verify mechanically, then continue with the grounding attached.** Never "I'm unsure, so I'll report
  and wait."
- Mechanical verification is cheap and decisive: on 2026-08-03 a security warning called the mirror's
  Jon-quotes unverifiable and **13 of 13 came back verbatim in minutes.**
