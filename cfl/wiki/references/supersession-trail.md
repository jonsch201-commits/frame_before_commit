## Definition

[verbatim] The citing page states the finding directly: "Professional's `wiki/references/supersession-trail.md` exists and is one of the six pages reachable by nothing. `[m 2026-08-24 ~23:3x, CFL: ABSENT. CFL has no supersession trail at all.]` Theirs is unreachable; mine does not exist." [paraphrase] A supersession trail, as the surrounding argument uses the term, is a record that a later CFL statement corrects an earlier one — explicitly distinguished from a bare "contradiction," because a correction and a differently-scoped-but-equally-true statement can look identical to a naive contradiction detector. The argument's point 3-4 is that CFL produces real corrections (it names three same-night examples: "12 to 6, 22 to 0, my own '3 of 3' to 2 of 3") with no trail recording them as corrections rather than contradictions, and that a detector built without one will flag CFL's own careful, qualified statements as defects.
