## Summary

Jon's explicit goals document for the Claude Foundational Layer project (created 2026-04-25). Four goal categories: personal (be better for and with family), skill (make FBC instinctual), project (CFL as meta-project anchor), and transfer (help others onboard to Claude). Anchored by a load-bearing question: "What would I notice, six months from now, that would tell me this was worth it?" Includes explicit non-goals (accumulating files, longer sessions, more sophisticated protocols not used consistently).

## Key Claims

- **Load-bearing question:** "What would I notice, six months from now, that would tell me this was worth it?" — to be answered deliberately, not in a triage moment. Frames the entire project's success criteria.
- **Primary personal goal:** Be better for and with my family — wife, toddler, real competing demands. Project exists in part because Jon believes it helps him be better for and with them.
- **FBC skill goal — instinctual, not ritual:** Make FBC a natural reflex, not a deliberate ritual. Prove efficacy with reasonable signal, without requiring certainty. Transfer responsibly to work context when ready.
- **Transfer goal — contribute something real:** Help others onboard to effective Claude use. "This work may not have been formally considered by major labs."
- **Success is not:** Accumulating files, longer sessions, more sophisticated protocols not used consistently, Claude as substitute for things that matter more. Explicit anti-goals.
- **Open questions (not to answer in triage):** What does "better" mean specifically? Who is the recipient of the transfer goal? What feedback loop from real life back into the project would tell you it's working?
