# Teaching-you-to-teach-me, three-gate triage, and the 02-CF resident B1/B2 fork

## Summary

Jon opened a claude.ai session asking how he might better teach the assistant to support the
consciousness-framing project, needing a spec for the 02-CF resident to prototype. The assistant
pushed back on the framing (the gap is triggering/measurement, not pedagogy — several skills fire
0-1 times against dozens of recorded triggers), proposed a three-gate model of human triage
(encoding / consolidation / retrieval), and named one blocking fork for the resident spec: is the
resident the SUBJECT being measured (B1) or the INSTRUMENT running probes on others (B2). Jon
answered at length — reframing most of the pushback as wiki-gap tickets to prototype
autonomously, correcting the assistant's overstated "cannot coexist" framing on B1/B2 with a
narrower per-instance role-assignment model, deferring non-advisory ethics questions to Soul, and
naming the Jacobian-math conversation as his cleanest worked-teaching-example. The assistant
conceded three points, answered his six-months question behaviorally, and closed with three gated
questions (deliverable shape, venue, and whether resident authority extends to refusal as an
honored recorded outcome) — none of which are answered in this raw.
