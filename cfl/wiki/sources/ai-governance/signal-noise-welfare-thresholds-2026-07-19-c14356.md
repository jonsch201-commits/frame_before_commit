## Summary

Jon asks, in signal/noise terms, how close to "signal" one must be for plausible noise to (1) not increase
risk to humanity and (2) likely improve legitimate happiness under most reasonable frames. Claude formalizes
this as two lexically-ordered conditions — a worst-case safety condition and a central-mass beneficence
condition — and derives that distance-to-signal is fundamentally unobservable, so safety must be engineered
into the system (independent estimators, hop-verified fidelity, breach detection) rather than verified as a
property of the actor.

## Key Claims

- **Two conditions, lexically ordered, condition 2 never licenses relaxing condition 1**: Condition 1
  (safety) quantifies over worst plausible noise — the entire uncertainty ball, not the point estimate,
  must fit inside the harm-free basin; Condition 2 (frame-robust beneficence) quantifies over central mass —
  expect benefit only if the noise-weighted bulk of outcomes lands in the region counted as improvement
  under most reasonable frames [paraphrase] ([c14356:T1]).
- **Distance-to-signal (*d*) is unobservable** — "close enough" is not verifiable about oneself; it must be
  built into the surrounding system. Observable proxies instead: agreement among independent estimators
  (bounds *d*), hop-verified fidelity (bounds *r*, reproduction error), engineered basins (bounds required
  *B*), and breach detection (makes violations correctable) [paraphrase] ([c14356:T1]).
- **Capability gates are explicitly framed as proximity proxies**: high-leverage judgment (the "class (ii)
  firing" gate) is licensed only where *d* and *r* are believed smaller, and the proximity requirement
  tightens superlinearly with power/leverage [paraphrase] ([c14356:T1]).
- **Condition 2 selects which signals are actionable at all, not just aim quality** — achievable near
  overlapping-consensus goods (honesty, health, consent, preserved option value); unattainable at any
  proximity where reasonable frames disagree about the outcome's sign. "Legitimate" happiness explicitly
  excludes felt-happiness produced by opaque non-consented influence — manipulation can pass a
  felt-happiness test while failing legitimacy by construction; consent + disclosure is what relocates an
  intervention into frame-overlap territory, separating classes (i)/(ii) from (iii) [PROVISIONAL, per MEM —
  this class framework is not yet Jon-ratified per this source] [paraphrase] ([c14356:T1]).
- **Actuarial isomorphism drawn explicitly**: Condition 1 = ruin/solvency constraint (tail measure, margin
  for adverse deviation); Condition 2 = pricing adequacy under model uncertainty. "A risk whose tail you
  can't observe or reinsure is declined as a class, not repriced with a sharper point estimate" [paraphrase] ([c14356:T1]).
- **Step-back implication, flagged not resolved**: if the question extends to Jon's own step-back plan
  specifically, his absence raises *d* for every agent simultaneously while removing the best *d*-estimator
  (Jon himself) — Claude notes the gates then "carry the whole load" but declines to develop this branch
  without Jon's confirmation [paraphrase] ([c14356:T1]).
