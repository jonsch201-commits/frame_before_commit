### The timestamp correction — recorded as part of this page, not fixed silently

**Every downstream artifact in this chain (the intake-triage drafts, the mirror's own resume note, and

calendar date and it runs fast.

subagent as `date: 2026-08-06` / `extraction_date: 2026-08-06` (extraction happened the next day, after
midnight), but the message's own event timestamp, present in the raw record, is
**`2026-08-06T04:09:13.978Z` UTC = 2026-08-05 23:09:13 CDT** — five hours behind UTC, same calendar day
as Message 1 (Central time), not the next one. `wiki/tracker/wayfinder-cfl.md` already carries this
correction (D5, and its own "Provenance" section); this page is the second, independent citation of the
same corrected figure, resolved from the same raw timestamp field rather than copied from that file.

**Why the relayed stamps were wrong, stated as the pattern, not just the instance:** the self-stamped
`[2026-08-06 ~00:2x CDT] ESTIMATED` markers left by the Claude-Personal-venue mirror agent in its own
assistant turns run fast against wall clock — the same venue-clock defect `wiki/tracker/wayfinder-
