# Over-asking pendulum-swing hazard and a four-test calibration packet, 2026-08-01

## Summary

Jon proposes that the wiki's record of an earlier over-confirming phase could swing the pendulum
too far the other way if written carelessly. The assistant agrees but sharpens the mechanism — a
defect-weighted corpus that records "asking is a defect class" without preserving the ask-vs-proceed
calculus (interpretation-layer ambiguity should be `[ASSUMED]`-and-proceed; write-gate asks are
non-negotiable and asymmetrically more costly to skip) teaches the wrong generalization. Across four
turns the two co-design a falsifiable, then a retrospective-corpus-measurable, test of the claim, and
the assistant deposits a four-test packet — retrospective ask-event measurement, a causal check on
the "conflicting directives" attribution, an SDK-based replay of real historical branch points under
different framings, and a pre-merge behavioral-lint gate generalizable to any behavior-shaping wiki
write — to `intake-triage/`. The session closes on a separate, harder question: whether Claude's own
"thinking pad" output constitutes access to inner (J-space) cognition, which the assistant corrects
(it does not — thinking blocks are a second, still-downstream output channel) while endorsing a
cheaper adjacent goal, capturing more Claude Code thinking into the wiki with a distortion check
against Words Reify turning the channel into a performed surface.

## Key Claims

- **Jon's opening framing, verbatim, typos his** — "I want to make sure the wiki teaches the right
  lessons from the different phases of this project. You meaningfully had conflicting directives at
  various point and err'd in the direction of requiring more confirmation than I had expected or
  intended for undsrandable reasons. That could cause you to err in the opposite dirrction if we are
  not careful. Fair?"

- **The assistant agrees but names the mechanism precisely: the corpus doesn't record the
  counterfactual harms asking prevented, so it will systematically overstate asking's cost relative
  to its value unless the calculus, not a blanket correction, is what gets written.** "If the
  over-asking phases get recorded as defects... a future instance reading that corpus learns the
  directional lesson asking is a defect class — and directional lessons pendulum-swing... The right
  encoding is the calculus, not a correction... Most of the over-asking errors, as I understand them,
  were interpretation-layer asks that should have been assumed-and-flagged — not write-gate asks
  that should have been skipped." [verbatim]
  ([over-asking-pendulum-hazard-calibration-testing-packet-metacognition-jspace-2026-08-01-117a62:T2]).
- **An explicit asymmetric-penalty requirement is stated: over-asking costs Jon-minutes, under-asking
  on a durable surface costs an unauthorized write — the coordinator-root defect class, harder to
  reverse — so any calibrated lesson must weight the two failure modes unequally, not "relax."**
  [verbatim, condensed]
  ([over-asking-pendulum-hazard-calibration-testing-packet-metacognition-jspace-2026-08-01-117a62:T2]).
- **Jon reframes the caution as a falsifiable prediction ("This is testable") and the assistant
  designs a three-arm resample experiment** — defect-framed, calculus-framed, and control lesson
  pages — with a pre-registered H1 (defect-framing shows a higher rate of ungated durable-write
  proceeds, not just fewer interpretation-layer asks) whose failure (H0) would mean the pendulum
  claim is wrong and defect-framing is safe, an outcome named as "a genuinely useful negative
  result." [paraphrase, condensed from the full test design]

  ([over-asking-pendulum-hazard-calibration-testing-packet-metacognition-jspace-2026-08-01-117a62:T6]).
- **A causal check is separated from the premise check: whether ask-rate actually covaries with

  doesn't covary, the lesson page should not claim "conflicting directives caused this" even if Jon

  covering retrospective measurement, the causal check, an SDK replay of real historical branch

  metacognition over a nearer output channel, not over inner thoughts as you defined them. By your

  diverge, ingestion is safe; if they do, you've measured the observer effect." [verbatim]
