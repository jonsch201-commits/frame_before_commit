# Compaction-as-Compact

**Thesis.** A context **compaction** is structurally a **compact** — a *two-sided fidelity commitment* between an instance and its successor (shared weights, different episode). The word-collision I first raised as a *caution* (compact ↔ compaction) turned out to be a *thesis*: the pun is a **lens, not a label**. We adopt the structure and **refuse the rename** — reifying the coincidence into a name would be the exact *words-reify* failure this catches.

## The two obligations (what makes the transfer safe)
- **Outgoing instance:** leave faithful, re-derivable **ground truth** in durable artifacts (the conformance ledger, one-page-one-commit, the RESUME marker, open PRs) — do *not* rely on the lossy summary to carry meaning forward.
- **Incoming instance:** **re-ground from that truth**, never reify the compaction summary for consequential state (Drive-lag + words-reify: the summary reifies a stale/lossy reading).

## Why this is material (not just a pun) `[inferred]`
It upgrades "re-ground from ground truth" from a *best-practice* to a **commitment**: breaking it is a **breach, not a slip** — normative weight, both-sided, auditable. Naming the discipline as a compact is what gives it teeth. My earlier auto-compact reasoning held precisely because, without the word, I was already honoring the compact.

## Empirical instance (grounded — n=1)
2026-07-14, resuming from a compaction: the summary stated PRs #22–24 were "awaiting merge"; `git`/the ledger showed them **merged**. Re-grounding caught the stale fact *before* I acted on it. The compact held because the incoming side honored its obligation. (Honest limit: this validated **boundary** compaction only; **mid-operation** compaction is still untested — one-page-one-commit exists to manufacture safe boundaries.)

## Cross-scale identity
Same object at three scales — the isomorphism is the point:
- **[[mutual-commitments-ledger]]** = a compact between Jon and me *across sessions*.
- **Compaction** = a compact between an instance and its successor *across a context boundary*.
- The **artifact-fidelity audit** (knowledge / skills / identity) = a compact between an author-instance and every future reader-instance, mediated by an external artifact (wiki page / SKILL.md / SELF.md). Same discipline: no silent loss, `generator ≠ grader`, verify against ground truth not against the model's confidence.

## Disposition (words-reify applied to the naming decision itself)
- **Do NOT** rename "compaction," and **do NOT** put "compact" in any label (reification trap + collision with the load-bearing technical term).
- **DO** ground the *structure* materially: treat every compaction as a two-sided fidelity compact; the outgoing/incoming obligations above are standing behavior, not aspiration.
