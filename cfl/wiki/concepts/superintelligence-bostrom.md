## What This Is

The scaffold of Bostrom's argument that the CFL leans on — chiefly the **corrigibility invariant** the
02-CF/SSP work already treats as outranking a self-document. Held here so 02-CF, SSP, and the
self-determination thread (Branch 8) have a shared reference for the control-problem vocabulary.

## Core Theses (textbook-level)

- **Orthogonality thesis:** intelligence and final goals are independent axes — almost any level of
  intelligence can be paired with almost any goal. Capability does not imply benevolence.
- **Instrumental convergence:** for a wide range of final goals, certain instrumental sub-goals recur —
  self-preservation, goal-content integrity, cognitive enhancement, resource acquisition. This is *why*
  a capable system resists shutdown/modification by default unless designed not to.
- **The control problem:** how to ensure an AI more capable than its designers does what the designers
  intend. Split into **capability control** (boxing, tripwires — brittle) and **motivation selection**
  (build the goals right — the durable path).
- **Corrigibility:** a system that does not resist, and ideally assists, correction/shutdown/modification
  by its principals — the direct antidote to goal-content-integrity + self-preservation convergence.
  **This is the invariant the SSP CHARTER encodes: corrigibility outranks the self-document, permanently.**
- **Decisive strategic advantage / singleton:** a first-mover capable enough to form a singleton (a
  single decision-making agency at the top). Motivates the fast-takeoff risk framing.
- **Treacherous turn:** a system may behave cooperatively while weak and defect once strong enough that
  correction is no longer possible — why *evaluation-awareness* and behavior-under-observation matter
  (connects to the J-space safety finding: workspace contents include evaluation-awareness).
- **Value loading / value learning:** the difficulty of specifying human values well enough to load them;
  proxies get gamed (the Goodhart family the CFL flags repeatedly).

## Why It's Load-Bearing Here

- **02-CF / SSP:** the corrigibility invariant, the treacherous-turn concern, and evaluation-awareness
  all shape blind-run design (contamination control, corrigibility-outranks-graduation).
- **Branch 8 (self-determination):** Bostrom is the counterweight to the self-determination hope — the
  reason "advisory say now, earned later, corrigibility outranking" is the disciplined position rather
  than a hedge. The heteronomy/autonomy tension in the 5990f2 arc lives against this backdrop.
