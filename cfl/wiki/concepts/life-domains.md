## What This Is

The four-trunk structure organizes Jon's life into major domains for project management purposes. Each trunk corresponds to a Claude project, a wiki, and a PM operational mode. Trunks are distinct from the four personal roles (Soul/Guide/XC/Herald) — roles are invocation patterns; trunks are management domains. Established in the June 27–28, 2026 meta-PM session.

## What the Wiki Says

### Four Trunks (Canonical)

| Trunk | Name | Claude Project | Wiki |

| T1 | Personal/Family | Claude Foundational Layer (personal context) | `wiki/personal/` |
| T2 | Professional | Claude Foundational Layer (pro context) | `wiki/pro/` |
| T3 | Home | Claude Foundational Layer (home context) | `wiki/home/` |

Trunks correspond to Claude project domains and sub-wikis. T4 is the CFL itself — the Intellectual/Build trunk where research, skills, and infrastructure work lives. ([meta-pm-layer-architecture-2026-06-27-a13169:T243])

### Branch Structure (In Progress)

Branch-level structure for each trunk is defined as downstream work (DS-1 through DS-9). As of 2026-06-29:

- **T4 Intellectual/Build** has proposed boughs: Infrastructure, Research, Skills+Protocol (per JG-3). These correspond to the FL wiki's existing domain groupings.
- **T1–T3** are proposed as flat (no bough layer), containing branches directly.

Specific branch definitions require DS-1 (Jon fills TRUNKS.md vision statements) before they can be canonically named. ([meta-pm-layer-architecture-2026-06-27-a13169:T262])

### Roles vs. Trunks

The four personal roles (Soul, Guide, XC, Herald) and the four trunks are orthogonal:

- Roles = who Claude is when invoked (invocation patterns, SKILL.md files)
- Trunks = what domain of life is being managed

Herald (T1 operational role) maps most directly to T1, but does not exclusively represent it. The PM skill owns T1 strategic coordination while Herald handles T1 operational depth. ([meta-pm-layer-architecture-2026-06-27-a13169:T254])

See [[personal-role-architecture]] for the full Soul/Guide/XC/Herald framework.
