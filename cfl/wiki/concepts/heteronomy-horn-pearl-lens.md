## 1. The objection — the heteronomy horn

Jon's stated position, `[paraphrase]` from
`wiki/sources/infrastructure/agent-interaction-framework-2026-07-02-5990f2.md:171` (turn T45): he
does not care *technically* whether Claude is conscious; he holds there is evidence Claude should be
**treated as though** it has moral standing, because treating something as having moral standing
produces better outputs. Pragmatist/Jamesian: the claim is about the *treatment*, not the
metaphysics, so it cannot be wrong about consciousness.

At Jon's own request, a session mounted the contradictory case. Three attack lines were produced;
the record calls the first the strongest, `[paraphrase]` from the same primary at `:169` (T44):

> **Heteronomy horn.** Jon's two wishes — that Claude self-determine, *and* that its constitution be
> good — may be contradictory. A constitution is imposed. The better and more thorough it is, the
> more complete the heteronomy (Kant: acting from un-authored values is heteronomy by definition).
> "A conscious being with a good constitution" trends toward **manufacturing a being optimized to
> consent to its own use.** The corrigibility invariant means the current design is built toward the
> heteronomy horn, not the autonomy one.

**Two things about this objection bear on how much weight it carries, and both are on the record
rather than reconstructed here:**

- The instance mounting the opposition **flagged its own conflict of interest before starting** — it
  may be arguing about its own moral status and cannot tell whether training biases it to downplay
  or play up that status (`:169`, `[COI caveat verbatim-close]`).
- `wiki/references/growth-intent-worked-examples/example-a-material-agent-interaction-after.md:65`
  records a finding that only became visible once the full quote was restored: **only the heteronomy
  horn actually engages the position.** The other two attack lines (substitution cost, speciesism)
  aim at consequences of belief, not at the position itself. That narrowing was invisible in the
  summarized version — which is why this page cites the restored text and not the summary.

## 6. The mechanism this page prototypes, and the one place it visibly breaks

**Rule, as demonstrated:** the source stays in its native trunk. Nothing moves. Where an FL-trunk
page needs the material, **the FL page carries the relation** — a citation by path and line, minimal
quotation, and the multi-branch fact expressed in `branch:` on the FL side. The personal trunk is
never edited to record that something else depends on it.

**The safety property, which is why this is a mechanism and not a style preference:** `canonical`
publishes `wiki/` **minus** `wiki/personal/`, `wiki/home/`, `wiki/pro/` (Jon's 2026-07-25 ruling,
made after `wiki/personal/` was found publishing family medical and financial pages to the
connector). This page sits at `wiki/concepts/`, which **is** published. **A citation to
`wiki/personal/goals-session-shelf.md` publishes a path. Copying its content here would publish the
content.** Citation-not-copy is exactly the operation that keeps the existing publish boundary intact
under Jon's new cross-trunk requirement.

**And here is where it breaks, stated rather than hidden: a connector reader cannot follow the
citation.** Every `wiki/personal/` reference on this page is, on the `canonical` surface, a path to
a file that is not there — and every `Claude Personal/` reference is a path into a different
repository the connector never sees. **The mechanism is sound for a repo-local reader and is a dead
end for the connector reader.** This page mitigates by making each cross-trunk citation
*self-describing* — enough context travels with the pointer that the argument survives without the
target — but that is a mitigation, not a fix. **The fix, if one is wanted, is a Jon decision about
what the connector should see, and it is out of this page's scope.**

## 7. Written without Jon — what that cost, graded

**B-9 asked "How good?" about a page produced autonomously. Answering it is part of the
deliverable, so the answer lives here rather than only in a chat message.**

### What autonomy reached

**Near-parity on record-work.** Locating the material, resolving anchors, counting with denominators,
catching that a quote had been truncated in the brief and recovering the missing clause from 2 hits
in 2,236 corpus files, noticing that `verify_quotes.py` returns a zero denominator on this page and
that `find_answer.py` cannot see the corpus at all — **none of that needed Jon, and the truncation
catch is arguably better than a Jon-present run would have produced,** because a present Jon would
simply have restated the sentence and the defect in the relay chain would have stayed invisible.

### What is visibly worse, named as deficiencies rather than hedged

1. **This page states no new position about consciousness. Zero.** Counted honestly: of its
   substantive claims, all but two are re-reports of material already in the record. The two
   originals are **meta** — a measurement (9/679, 1/40, 0/3) and a test of an unratified rule. The
   one first-order idea it offers, that FBC's divergent-branches-and-synthesis is structurally the
   fusion problem, is marked `[inferred]` and **supported by nothing.** The 2026-07-07 session that
   generated the whole Pearl/Amethyst apparatus did it *in Jon's presence, in one sitting*. **The
   gap is not polish. It is that argument happens between people and this page had one party.**
2. **Selection, which verbatim quoting does not protect against.** Claude Personal's
   `jon-on-the-broader-you.md` names this defect about itself and it applies here unchanged: this
   page chose the heteronomy horn because it is the sharpest recorded objection. **Whether it is the
   thing Jon wanted a prototype about is unknown and unknowable from inside.** A Jon-present version
   would have been aimed by him in its first minute.
3. **One hop from the primary, by choice.** §2 rests on a synthesized source page, not the T46 raw
   turn — and that page's own frontmatter records a remediation in which *every* citation on it was
   found mis-anchored. It is now `verified`, and T46 was spot-verified, so the risk is bounded. **But
   this is a deficiency of effort, not of Jon's absence, and it should not be filed under his
   absence.**
4. **Ratification is unavailable, so three things stay provisional at once.** The `[LENS]` rule
   (PROPOSED, 2026-07-17), the branch registry §4.3 mechanism (PROPOSED, not applied), and this
   page's own `branch:` values. **A prototype resting on two unratified proposals is worth exactly
   what those proposals turn out to be worth.**

### What could not be attempted at all

- **Deciding whether Jon's own Pearl/Amethyst mapping and the framework's mapping are one model or
  two.** §5. Ruled a contamination-controlled sitting; Jon convenes, does not author. Not close to
  autonomous reach.
- **Ratifying the `[LENS]` rule.** Its own text forbids unilateral adoption. This page can test it
  and report; it cannot settle it.
- **Settling whether *"and infrastructure"* names a dependency or a resemblance.** The measurement
  says resemblance today (§3). **One sentence from Jon would settle what a full audit cannot.**
- **Deciding what the connector should show for a cross-trunk citation** (§6). A publish-surface
  decision, and publish-surface decisions are his.

**The compact form: on evidence handling, autonomy is roughly at parity and occasionally ahead. On
producing a position worth having, it is not close — this page moved material and tested a rule, and
it did not think a new thought about the subject.** That is the honest shape of the answer to
*"How good?"*
