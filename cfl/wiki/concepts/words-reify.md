## JON-GATE: proposed wording — superseded by R4 above, kept for provenance only

**Superseded 2026-07-28.** This block was the agent's best guess while no Jon-sourced intent
existed. It no longer governs — the "Jon's Stated Intent" section above does. Retained verbatim
below so the delta between the guess and Jon's actual words is checkable, per this concept's own
rule against silently overwriting a prior (even if superseded) claim.

The concept's own rule applies reflexively: any account of *what words-reify means or implies* beyond
Jon's verbatim quotes above is this agent's reconstruction, and per Jon's 2026-07-06 instruction that
reconstruction must not be written into this page as his formulation. The following is offered only as
a **bracketed, non-canonical restatement** for Jon to confirm, edit, or reject — it is not asserted as
his position:

> [JON-GATE: proposed wording — awaiting Jon] "Words reify" holds that for both humans and AI, the act
> of naming or committing a claim to writing changes its epistemic status — it stops being tentative and
> starts being retrievable, citable, and inherited as ground truth by later readers who have no access to
> the uncertainty the writer actually held. The consequence for this wiki specifically: a material commit
> to `wiki/` is not neutral record-keeping — whatever epistemic state produced a sentence (grounded
> finding, plausible instinct, unresolved speculation), the sentence can be read by every future session
> as settled fact unless its epistemic status is marked in the text itself. For this wiki in particular,
> that means: (1) a claim's confidence/fidelity should be tagged inline at the point of writing, not held
> only in the writer's head; (2) coining a term or label is a load-bearing act, not a stylistic choice,
> because the label will be retrieved and built on; (3) summarizing is not epistemically free — a summary
> can manufacture certainty a raw turn never had.

The three sub-points above track claims the 2026-07-24 aggregation packet proposed. They are grounded in
the 07-06 Fable exchange Jon pasted (the paste-ready insert enumerated inline epistemic tagging, deliberate
naming, and flagging summary-vs-decision) ([code-2026-07-06-da51cc-cc-handoff-packet-review]) and in
T-126's gloss ([chat-2026-07-11-090a56-master-of-triage]) — but the packet's synthesis into a single
"definition" is this agent's/Fable's reading, not a verbatim Jon statement, so it stays inside the
JON-GATE block until Jon ratifies, amends, or replaces it.
