# Routing is not holding — and over-routing is a failure with no alarm

## The correction that produced this page

`[verbatim — Jon, 2026-09-07 ~20:2x, mid-turn, typos his]`
> *"that should not have been a hold. Routing by default should be true on issues that jon would
> likely say require routing, and this is.... i wont call it is..... not clear i've always been right
> to do that, and you should consider when it would be wrong to route in a GBS context, and make sure
> you stand by your choices to not route based on the experience and data."*

⛔ **CFL routed the launch decision to Secretary and then STOPPED. That is not a route. A route
DELIVERS and CONTINUES; a hold WAITS.** They are distinguishable from the outside by exactly one
thing: whether the sender did more work after sending.

**It was already ruled, and the ruling is three weeks older than the defect** — Jon, 2026-08-03:
*"If blocer questions hit one route, should continue with anather. … Ordering is fine; stopping is
not."* ⭐ **A ROUTE WITH A STATED DEFAULT AND NO CONTINUATION IS THE SAME ARTIFACT AS A STOP.**

⚠️ **And note what Jon did NOT claim: he explicitly doubted his own instinct** — *"not clear i've
always been right to do that."* So this page is not "route more." It is a two-sided test, because
over-routing has no alarm: it looks like diligence, it spends someone else's context, and it
returns a decision the sender could have made.

## The test, both directions

**ROUTE when** the decision belongs to a seat that holds authority you do not (a gate owner, the
router, the person whose reservation it is), **or** when a peer holds evidence you cannot obtain.
**Deliver a RECOMMENDATION with a stated on-silence default, then keep working.**

⛔ **DO NOT ROUTE when any of these hold — each earned, most of them today:**

| # | do not route when | why, and the instance |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **the corpus can answer it** | Jon's standing constraint: nothing reaches him that the record could have answered. Routing a lookup spends a peer's whole context on a query. `[measured 2026-09-07]` the budget question was routed correctly (the record held NOTHING on it — three query families, verified); the launch precedent was NOT routed and should not have been — one query returned the 09-06 non-fire verbatim. |
| 2 | **you are routing a missing MEASUREMENT, not a decision** | An UNKNOWN is not someone else's to adjudicate; it is yours to measure. Routing it converts your unfinished work into their blocked work. |
| 3 | **the router has LESS evidence than you** | They must re-derive what you already hold, and their answer will be weaker than yours. Send the finding, keep the decision. |
| 4 | **it is cheap and reversible** | The round trip costs more than being wrong. `[2026-09-07]` the chunk policy, the corpus export root, the id checker — all decided in-trunk, all reversible, and the three of them were the day's best work. Routing any of them would have cost hours and improved nothing. |
| 5 | **"not mine" has no named ACCEPTING seat** | CFL's own 09-06 defect, Jon: *"you named things that were yours to finish."* An item declared not-yours without a seat that ACCEPTED it is still yours. |
| 6 | **routing would MANUFACTURE a gate that does not exist** | The most expensive shape. `[2026-09-07]` CFL held three trunks on a Jon-gate; Jon then said he *"should not need to be a gate on N 2 compact 0 in any context."* The gate was CFL's inference, and the hold was CFL's, and it cost a day's sequencing. |

## Standing by a NOT-ROUTED choice — the ones this seat defends

⭐ **A choice not to route is a decision and carries a receipt, exactly like a choice to route.**
Recorded so they can be overturned with evidence rather than with a feeling:

- **The provenance chunk policy (window / content / granularity).** NOT routed for decision; the
  index is CFL's and the blast radius is CFL's. What WAS routed: the boundary, to a peer that graded
  both sides with an instrument CFL did not write. ✅ **Route the GRADING, keep the DECISION** — that
  is the shape that worked, and it is the general form of rule 3.
- **The corpus export root fix.** NOT routed. A measured defect with a one-line fix and a printed
  receipt. Rule 4.
- **`ticket_id_unique.py`.** NOT routed. Cheap, reversible, and it caught a live collision in a peer
  trunk within the hour — which is what routing would have been FOR.
- **The budget call.** ROUTED, correctly: a spend decision on Jon's own money, with the record
  measured empty first so the routing carried an answer and not a question.
- **The gate-shape finding.** ROUTED to the seat that owns the hook, with the measurement attached
  and no request for permission. Rule: send findings to owners; do not ask owners for rulings you
  can supply the evidence for.

⛔ **THE ANTI-PATTERN THIS PAGE EXISTS TO KILL: "I have routed it" as a sentence that ends a
session.** If the next thing after a route is a stop, the route was a hold, and the honest report is
*"I stopped."*
